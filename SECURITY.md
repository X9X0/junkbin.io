# Security Policy

Junkbin.io is built by and for hackers and makers. If you find a security
issue, we'd genuinely rather hear it from you first than find out the hard
way — that's the whole point of this file.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't open a public GitHub issue for security problems.**

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting instead:

1. Go to the [Security tab](../../security) of this repository.
2. Click **"Report a vulnerability"**.
3. Describe the issue, how to reproduce it, and its impact as best you
   understand it.

This opens a private conversation with the maintainer — nobody else can see
the report until we agree it's safe to discuss publicly.

## What to expect

- We'll acknowledge new reports within a few days.
- We'll keep you posted as we investigate and fix the issue.
- We'll credit you (if you want credit) once it's resolved, unless you'd
  rather stay anonymous.

## Scope

In scope: junkbin.io itself (frontend, backend/API, and this repository's
code) and anything that could compromise user accounts, data, or the
integrity of the site.

Out of scope: third-party services we depend on (report those to the
vendor directly), and issues that require physical access to someone's own
already-unlocked device.

## Safe harbor

If you're testing in good faith, sticking to your own account (or a test
account you created), and reporting responsibly through the channel above,
we're not going to come after you for it. Please don't test against other
users' accounts or data without their permission.

## Past reports

We'll list resolved issues here once they've shipped and had time to reach
everyone, so anyone curious can see what's been found and fixed.
